## 1. ANAE legal and activity framework

### 1.1 Confirmed framework

- **Current statute:** `Loi n° 22-23` is the current auto-entrepreneur statute. Older product notes that referred to `Loi n° 15-22` are not the current framework. `VERIFIED FACT` — official ANAE/JORADP instruments reviewed 2026-11-20.
- **Implementing decrees identified:** `Décret n° 23-196`, `Décret n° 23-197`, `Décret n° 23-198`, `Décret n° 24-76`, and `Décret n° 25-170`. `VERIFIED FACT` as identified instrument numbers; specific article-level assertions are limited to the text below.
- **ANAE card QR requirement:** The QR element on the auto-entrepreneur card is in `Décret n° 23-198`. `VERIFIED FACT`.
- **Official activity catalogue:** ANAE publishes a bilingual French/Arabic activity catalogue at `https://activities.anae.dz/`. `VERIFIED FACT` — retrieved 2026-11-20.

## 2. Algerian FX and payment rules for digital/IT service exports

### 2.1 Confirmed-law table

| # | Issue | Rule as of 2026-11-20 | Status / source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | Service exports generally | Règlement 07-01 Art. 57, as amended by Règlement 2021-01: contract domiciliation, collection, and repatriation rules for service exports are the same as for goods exports. | `VERIFIED FACT` — Règlement 2021-01 Art. 2. |
| A2 | Digital/start-up/non-commercial-professional exemption | Exemption from bank-domiciliation formalities for online exports of digital services, start-up services, and services of non-commercial professionals. | `VERIFIED FACT` — Règlement 2021-01 Art. 2. No official definition located for the listed categories: `UNKNOWN`. |
| A3 | Declaration duty | Exempt providers must lodge a declaration with the domiciliary bank describing project(s), unit price, and date put online. | `VERIFIED FACT` — Règlement 2021-01 Art. 2. Official template/filing mechanics not located: `UNKNOWN`. |
| A4 | Repatriation and FX-account crediting | Payments for exported services must be repatriated to a bank in Algeria and credited to the exporter’s FX merchant or non-commercial-professional account, used for the activity. | `VERIFIED FACT` — Règlement 2021-01 Art. 2. |
| A5 | Undeclared/non-domiciled/late receipts | Undeclared-project receipts are collected in DZD; Art. 67 and Instruction 06-2021 Art. 4 also address non-domiciled and late receipts in DZD. | `VERIFIED FACT` — Règlement 2021-01 Arts. 2 and 5; Instruction 06-2021 Art. 4. | A6 | Current repatriation deadline | Règlement 26-02 of 23 Jul 2026, JO n°58 of 12 Aug 2026, amends Art. 61: non-hydrocarbon export proceeds must be repatriated within 120 days from shipment or date of service performance; where payment term exceeds 120 days, up to 180 days with prior export-credit insurance and express contract term. | `VERIFIED FACT` — Règlement 26-02 Art. 2. ### 2.2 Banking practice and official guidance

- **BOA 48-hour domiciliation note**, D.G.C n°2141/2026 of 28 Jul 2026, reminds banks that domiciliation must be completed within 48 hours of a complete file under Art. 29 of Règlement 07-01. It does not expressly repeal the Art. 57 digital-services domiciliation exemption. `VERIFIED FACT` for text; `DOCUMENTED INTENT` for policy; `INFERENCE` for non-repeal.
- **BOA “100%” guidance**, 18 Jul 2021: exporters have access to 100% of non-hydrocarbon export proceeds, with business-import use and up to 20% for export promotion. `DOCUMENTED INTENT` / `VERIFIED FACT`.
- **BOA authorised-products catalogue**, 14 May 2026: lists bank cards, international cards, foreign-card acceptance for Algerian web merchants, and mobile/QR payments; no third-party foreign virtual-USD wallet is listed. `VERIFIED FACT` as to the catalogue; `INFERENCE` that foreign fintech wallets are not equivalent to BOA-authorised products.
- **Instruction 08-2026**, effective 10 Aug 2026: DZD short-term export working-capital finance for registered operators in specified activity codes under documentary credit/collection. `VERIFIED FACT`; `INFERENCE` that ordinary digital freelancers generally fall outside its stated eligibility.
- **Ministry of Commerce FAQ**: IBS exemption applies proportionally to export-generating FX operations, including service exports, subject to proof of FX remittance to an Algerian bank. `VERIFIED FACT` as official guidance; not tax advice.

### 2.3 Interpretation and unresolved questions

- Classification of online software/DevOps/design/data/consulting work as `services numériques` or `professionnel non commerçant` is plausible but not bindingly defined in the reviewed texts. `INFERENCE` / `UNKNOWN`.
- Declaration template, portal, update deadline, and treatment of marketplace/platform invoices were not located. `UNKNOWN`.
- Whether a properly declared, domiciliation-exempt digital export counts as “domiciled” for FX-crediting purposes is unclear. `UNKNOWN`.
- Holding export proceeds in a foreign fintech wallet or foreign virtual-account balance does not, on the face of the text, satisfy repatriation to a bank in Algeria. `VERIFIED FACT` for rule; `INFERENCE` for application; `UNKNOWN` for any specific structure.
- Travel FX cards are for the allowance regime, not export-revenue collection. `INFERENCE` for product use; `VERIFIED FACT` for misuse prohibitions.
- Crypto-linked settlement requires product-specific official classification against Loi 25-10 Art. 6 bis. `VERIFIED FACT` for prohibition; `UNKNOWN` for product-specific application.

### 2.4 RedotPay / Grey treatment

- No official Algerian approval, agrément, or annual PSP-list evidence was located for RedotPay or Grey. `UNKNOWN`.
- The only official 2026 PSP item located was the 23 Sep 2026 authorisation for Djezzy Payment Services to be constituted as a PSP, with later agrément application due within 12 months; it is not RedotPay or Grey and not a full operating agrément. `VERIFIED FACT`.
- Grey’s own public marketing describes multi-currency accounts and virtual cards; that is provider marketing, `OWNER-PROVIDED CONTEXT` / unverified, not evidence of Algerian authorisation or legality.
- No official Algerian source for RedotPay was located. To the extent any flow involves virtual assets, Loi 25-10 Art. 6 bis must be considered; product-specific application is `UNKNOWN`.
- **Product stance:** do not represent RedotPay or Grey as licensed in Algeria, bank-approved, partnered with an Algerian bank, or as satisfying repatriation. `STRATEGIC PROPOSAL`.
- **Compliant-by-design posture:** declare the project, contract/invoice in the exporter’s name, require settlement by bank transfer to an Algerian FX merchant/non-commercial-professional account, retain repatriation proof, and obtain written bank confirmation for classification and FX crediting. `STRATEGIC PROPOSAL`, not legal advice.
