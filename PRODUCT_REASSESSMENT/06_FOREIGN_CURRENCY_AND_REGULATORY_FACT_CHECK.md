**Assessment date:** 2026-11-20.
**Scope:** Official-source fact check for product positioning around Algerian ANAE digital professionals, cross-border digital service work, PSP/payment wording, and foreign-currency claims.

## 4. RedotPay and Grey

`UNKNOWN:` No official Algerian approval, agrément, annual PSP-list evidence, or bank-partnership evidence was located for **RedotPay** or **Grey** on `bank-of-algeria.dz` or `joradp.dz` during the 2026-11-20 research pass.

`VERIFIED FACT:` The only official 2026 PSP item located was the 23 Sep 2026 authorisation for Djezzy Payment Services to be constituted as a PSP, with later agrément application due within 12 months. It is neither RedotPay nor Grey and is not a full operating agrément.

`OWNER-PROVIDED CONTEXT / UNVERIFIED PROVIDER MARKETING:` Grey’s own public marketing describes multi-currency accounts and virtual cards. This is provider marketing, not evidence of Algerian authorisation or legality.

`VERIFIED FACT / UNKNOWN:` No official Algerian source for RedotPay was located. To the extent any flow involves virtual assets, Loi 25-10 Art. 6 bis must be considered; product-specific application is `UNKNOWN`.

`STRATEGIC PROPOSAL:` Do not represent RedotPay or Grey as licensed in Algeria, bank-approved, partnered with an Algerian bank, or as satisfying repatriation.

`STRATEGIC PROPOSAL:` Do not build in-product claims that either provider makes an Algerian digital professional compliant. Refer the professional to the bank and official sources.

## 5. Product boundary

`VERIFIED FACT:` The current Codexx source has no financial wallet, ledger, payout, invoice, FX, payment, or wallet-address concept. The Public Professional Wallet is a presentation and inquiry surface, not a financial wallet.

`STRATEGIC PROPOSAL:` Keep Codexx free of:
- balances, ledgers, payouts, or stored value;
- crypto/virtual-asset settlement assumptions;
- claims that a foreign fintech wallet satisfies Algerian repatriation;
- payment-facilitation language that could be mistaken for PSP activity;
- unverified provider names in trust or compliance badges.

`STRATEGIC PROPOSAL:` If cross-border service work is discussed in Academy/enablement content, keep it educational and reference official instruments with dates. Do not provide individualized legal, tax, or banking advice.

`STRATEGIC PROPOSAL:` A compliant-by-design posture would be: declare the project, contract/invoice in the exporter’s name, require settlement by bank transfer to an Algerian FX merchant or non-commercial-professional account, retain repatriation proof, and obtain written bank confirmation for classification and FX crediting. This is product guidance, not legal advice.
