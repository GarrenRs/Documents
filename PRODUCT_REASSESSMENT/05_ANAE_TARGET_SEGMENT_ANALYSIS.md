## 1. Owner-provided segment definition

`OWNER-PROVIDED CONTEXT:` Codexx Academy should serve **Algerian ANAE self-employed digital professionals** within a professional digital work environment. The four interfaces are the public Academy landing page, Administration Dashboard, Member Dashboard/Private Workspace, and Public Professional Wallet.

`OWNER-PROVIDED CONTEXT:` The target is not a generic freelancer population and not every holder of a digital skill. It is a professionally registered auto-entrepreneur whose activity is compatible with digital/IT service work.

`STRATEGIC PROPOSAL:` Treat ANAE status as a **trust signal and onboarding criterion**, not as an automatic marketing claim. Codexx has no evidenced ANAE endorsement or partnership.

`UNKNOWN:` ANAE endorsement, partnership, or data-sharing authorisation for Codexx.

## 6. Trust and verification guardrails

`STRATEGIC PROPOSAL:` Any future ANAE-oriented verification should follow these guardrails:

1. Tie eligibility to official activity codes and labels from the ANAE catalogue; do not invent categories.
2. Distinguish clearly between **self-declared** professional data, **owner-reviewed** evidence, and **officially verified** ANAE status.
3. Never state or imply ANAE partnership, endorsement, or directory integration without written evidence.
4. Do not treat a public portfolio, a theme, or three projects as proof of ANAE registration.
5. Keep QR-card work tied to the official card framework and owner authorisation; do not fabricate an ANAE credential.
6. Require Arabic/RTL review for any target-facing identity or verification text.
7. Record verification decisions and review basis in the product’s governance model; keep them auditable.
8. Avoid legal conclusions. Refer professionals to official ANAE and bank sources for eligibility, tax, and banking questions.

`UNKNOWN:` The owner’s intended evidence standard for an “ANAE verified” badge or profile state.

---

## 7. Commercial and product implications

`INFERENCE:` A narrow ANAE-digital segment can support a stronger trust proposition than a generic audience because official activity codes give a defensible vocabulary for positioning. However, the value must come from **verified proof, professional presentation, direct inquiry, enablement, and governed administration**, not from claiming ANAE authority.

`STRATEGIC PROPOSAL:` Prioritise, in order:
1. stable onboarding and professional profile editing;
2. ANAE activity-code/self-declaration with owner-controlled review states;
3. public wallet repair (SEO, mobile, Arabic/RTL, project-detail error);
4. Academy enablement content with clear ownership;
5. governed announcements/inquiries instead of unsafe member-to-member threads;
6. QR identity-card design only after owner/official boundaries are settled;
7. external-platform connectors only after legal and operational review.

`STRATEGIC PROPOSAL:` Do not build financial wallet, payout, FX, or crypto-settlement features into this segment proposition. See `06`.
